AI Discovery Framework

A lightweight way to surface bottom-up efficiencies and inform top-down corporate strategy.

1. The Goal

Run a focused set of discovery sessions that deliver value on two levels at the same time:

  • Bottom-up: identify quick efficiency wins in daily workflows that staff feel immediately.
  • Top-down: spot patterns across teams (shared data bottlenecks, common pain points, capability gaps) that inform corporate strategy.
The sweet spot: high user frustration meets low technical complexity. Quick wins prove value. Patterns across those wins shape the longer-term roadmap.

3. Discovery Questions (Pick 5 for the workshop)

Pain Points

  • What is the most frustrating, repetitive task your team does daily?
  • Where do processes stall while waiting on sign-offs or info?
  • Which questions flood your inbox but need little thinking?

Quick Wins

  • Where would a 10% to 20% speed gain change your week?
  • Do you have good internal documents staff struggle to search?

Wasted Time

  • How long do users spend searching across different systems?
  • Which templates or reports do you redraft over and over?

Readiness

  • What is the cleanest dataset we could access right away?
  • Who are the super users keen to test a prototype?
  • What manual process is already mapped step by step?

4. The Scoring Framework

Rate each idea from 1 to 5 on these four criteria. Keep it fast: 5 minutes per idea.

Impact Score (Value Created)

B. Frustration ReducedMinor nuisanceAnnoying, regular complaintsUniversally hated task

Impact Total = A + B (out of 10)

Complexity Score (Ease of Delivery)

CriterionScore 1 (Hard)Score 3 (Medium)Score 5 (Easy)
C. Data ReadinessMessy, paper, legacy silosDigital but needs cleaningClean, centralised, templated
D. Risk & IntegrationRegulated data, writes to core systemsSensitive data, human reviewsLow-risk, read-only assistant

Ease Total = C + D (out of 10). A higher score means easier to build.

6. From Quick Wins to Corporate Strategy

The discovery sessions produce two complementary outputs. Both are valuable to the executive team.

Bottom-Up: Tactical WinsTop-Down: Strategic Themes
  • Specific tools delivered to specific teams.
  • Hours saved, frustration reduced, morale lifted.
  • First prototype in 4 weeks, not 4 months.
  • Low-risk, read-only or draft-only tools to start.
  • Common data bottlenecks across business units.
  • Recurring capability gaps (e.g. search, drafting, integration).
  • Shared governance and access blockers.
  • Evidence to shape investment priorities and the AI roadmap.
The principle: every workshop produces two outputs. A practical win for the team in the room, and a data point for the executive view. Patterns across sessions become the corporate strategy.
